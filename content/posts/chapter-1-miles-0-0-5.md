<p>This is entry 1 to my series of updates about my PCT hike.(A continuous footpath walking&nbsp;2650+ miles through California, Washington, and Oregon going from the Mexican to Canadian border).&nbsp; Throughout these entries you will follow along with me as I adventure, struggle, learn and grow from my quest.&nbsp; I also hope to write lots of other things in addition to my updates, so in addition to following my hike you get to (or suffer to) be exposed to what is churning in my strange noggin as I walk.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>As another, even stranger guy said &#8220;All truly great thoughts are conceived of by walking&#8221;      </p><p>-Nietzsche</p><p>Alas, you are here to hear about my quest!&nbsp;</p><p>It all began in midtown Atlanta where I almost lost my sanity.</p><p>There in that horrible place I swore to myself that in reward for my finishing that corporate summer I would go on a ridiculously difficult and grand adventure the next summer&#8212;one that would clear my head (and liver) from college and launch me into the many adventures and (figurative) mountains of life with the fullness of my soul, body and mind.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>I will talk more about my mind and motivations later but the physical journey really began on Tuesday May 19th.&nbsp; I woke up at 5:00am and came downstairs to hug my mom goodbye and then give my Dad a firm handshake! (And then hug him too).&nbsp; My Mom dropped me off at the airport with a grocery bag full of fresh sausage balls and my sister&#8217;s chocolate cookies.&nbsp; [savored last home food!]</p><p>At the airport I met my friend from high school John. John is one of my oldest friends in the world, a hard worker, apparently something of a clean freak, and is going into Real Estate Banking in July.&nbsp; John is coming out to do the first 180 miles with me.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Eventually we landed in San Diego which, suprisingly&#8230;&#8230; I kinda loved.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>They had&nbsp;</p><p>Aircraft carriers, pirate ships, and beers on the
