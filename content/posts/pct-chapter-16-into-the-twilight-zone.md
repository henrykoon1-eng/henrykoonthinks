<p>As I write the rain is pouring, the thunder is rolling, and somehow the fires are still burning just a hundred miles north. Yesterday I took a day to rest/wait out some of the storm and get myself a replacement sleeping pad (my old one is permanently deflated &#128546;). Last night I spent the evening in the towns bar with some lumberjacks and wildfire fighters.  Today I ate 3 breakfasts, bought new food, and have decided I&#8217;m incapable of waiting any longer and will be venturing out into the storm.  I am already 1/3 into Washington, it&#8217;s crazy to think this will probably be the second (or third) to last chapter of this trip. </p><p>Washington is stunningly beautiful, I am in good spirits It was full of very nice people and they were incredibly welcoming and kind.  But I must say it was so very much a &#8220;Boomer&#8221; church.  The woman leading the worship stopped the congregation several times to change the lyrics from &#8220;Christians&#8221; to &#8220;God&#8217;s Children&#8221; in order to be inclusive for her husband who is a Rabbi.  There was also the shaman of a local Buddhist temple in the congregation.  The preacher was going on a 3 week vacation and his &#8220;flock was to be watched&#8221; by these three avowedly non Christian leaders.  And of course there were very few young people there.  During the service there was one extended segment of sharing the microphone to give thanks (which clearly became a bragging contest veneered under false humility) and then a segment of personal prayers (which was exclusively for the minor surgeries and health issues of the aging congregation).  I have thoughts on the effect of this very &#8220;Boomer&#8221; attitude which I have found at every one of the mainline Protestant churches I have stopped at.  I should write a separate essay on it but for now I will just say this.  1. The unwillingness to define a thing prevents it from existing concretely: by definition a country without borders cannot be a country, by definition a religion without beliefs is just a club or friend group.  2.  Obviously old people have health issues and the Bible does say that priests and congregations should pray over their sick.  But the primary healing of the hospital that the church is must be the healing of souls damaged by sin&#8212;these wounds cannot be healed by ignoring sin or by prolonging the physical life.  The generation of skepticism, of the hippies, of the anti-war movement, and of religious skepticism has failed to tear down those institutions it first called corrupt, it has only weakened them.  Neither the government nor the church is less corrupt but the nation is un-pious and unpatriotic. ((But hopefully more on this later, (and of course I love all the boomers in my life, there&#8217;s just a reason their churches and institutions are crumbling through their smiles))  After church they (easily) made me eat lots of birthday cake and ice cream.  (We sung happy birthday to 2 different members in the service)  </p><p> ANYWAYS&#8230;after church I began climbing up towards Mt. Adams.  I spent much of this hike trying to ship a replacement sleeping pad to my next town and didn&#8217;t make it very far.  Nonetheless I slept beneath Mt.
