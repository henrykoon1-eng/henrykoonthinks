<p>Day 5.&nbsp; Leaving Julian we had a famous Trail Angel (someone who helps hikers) named &#8220;The Professor&#8221; [a beautifully strange man and is certainly autistic]  scheduled to give us a ride at 8:00.&nbsp; So me, John, Tim, and Frenchie went ahead and got another meal in town to start us off.&nbsp; I crushed my second Chicken and Waffles of the stay and was almost moved to tears again. (Mainly because they give a thorough relapse to my butter addiction)</p><p>On the trail we began the long and gradual ascent again.&nbsp; On this occasion, for the first time since the start of the trip we put in headphones.&nbsp; I am proud we made it 5 days of just talking, but man did it feel good to have music for a brutal uphill.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>-With the combination of music and a full
