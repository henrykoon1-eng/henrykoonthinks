<p>I write this entry not because it has been so long since my last entry, in fact you will probably receive this at the same time, but because I have had some thoughts.&nbsp;</p><p>Anyways, the past few days since Kackie left have been new, interesting and I think worth sharing.&nbsp;</p><p>Day 54. [944-964]</p><p>{I know this is a repeat day but there is more to say}</p><p>In addition to (&#8220;big&#8221;) Troy, I also hiked with &#8220;Cliffhanger&#8221; the Australian actor, entrepreneur, and filmmaker from Hollywood. He is writing a tv show about the trail (as I&#8217;m sure many think they are) and is quite confident in it. He gave a big speech about how people shouldn&#8217;t talk politics on the trail&#8212;-as he made his way into a long rant about his hate for Trump. &#8212;-&gt;I, as usual, agreed with more informed and equally passionate disapproval of him but from the position of one betrayed and not opposed.&nbsp;</p><p>&#8230;anyways, he got too crazy so I ditched him and hiked past him. But there are a lot of people &#8220;in the film industry&#8221; on the trail and I thought I would share.&nbsp;</p><p>Also, today, for the first time in the trail, it began to rain.
