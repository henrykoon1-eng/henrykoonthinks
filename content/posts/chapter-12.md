bear, I hiked through a semi-active volcano, and I was briefly adopted by a family on vacation.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>The trail has not been as pretty, it has been hot, dry, dusty, and absolutely overrun by thorn bushes.&nbsp; </p><p>&#8212;But as they say in little league football&#8230;.      &#8220;No pain, no gain&#8221;</p><div><hr></div><p><strong>Day 63 [1154.5-1170.5]</strong></p><p>When last
