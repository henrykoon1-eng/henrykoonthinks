<p>Hello friends, I hope all is well with you. The trail is certainly trailing&#8230;</p><p><em>-the notes in italics are (some of) my thoughts from each day.&nbsp;&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>-Note the second person &#8220;you&#8221; is addressed to me, I am not lecturing my readers.</em></p><p><strong>Day 57 [1018-1033.2]</strong></p><p>I woke up in the bushes at Kennedy Meadows North, where I had &#8220;stealth camped&#8221; the night before, covered in dew from the adjacent stream.  I spent the morning drying out my gear (and brain) from the dew and conversations of the night before. Then I got a hitch with a nice &#8220;Dave&#8221; who wears driving gloves and a cap, back to the trailhead.&nbsp;</p><p>I made it 15 miles through some absolutely stunning wildflowers and had a beautiful campsite.&nbsp;</p><p><em>Sometimes the sun will start to set before you reach camp and you will have to decide whether to stop and watch it or race ahead to catch the end of it.
